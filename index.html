Good to know

  • Staple ★ songs go in every set, automatically.
  • Type a number in # of Originals or # of Covers and it never goes over that number.
  • Change Guitar tuned and the capo and key notes on each song adjust for you.
  • Use Add a song… under the set to drop one more in.
  • On a phone, tap the browser's share button and choose Add to Home Screen to use it like an app.
