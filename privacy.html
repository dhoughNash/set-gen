Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Set Gen ("the App") is a song catalog and set list generator operated by Now You Know Nashville LLC of Nashville, Tennessee ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account information. Your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form; we cannot see them.
  • Your content. Songs you add (title, tempo, original/cover, length, intro length, mood, voicing, capo, tuning, staple status) and set lists you save (name, show or venue, date, and song order).
  • Account access. A record that your account has access to Set Gen on our shared sign-in platform.
  • Subscription information. Whether you are on the free plan or Pro, and an identifier that links your account to Stripe, our payment processor.
  • On-device data. Your current working set list and your guitar-tuning preference are stored in your browser's local storage. The App also caches its own pages on your device through a service worker so it can load faster and work offline.

We do not collect or store your payment card details. If you buy Pro, you enter them directly with Stripe. We do not collect precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the App does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store your songs and set lists so they are available across your devices.
  • To process and manage Pro subscriptions and enforce plan limits.
  • To operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the App, and to contact you about your account or the App.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your songs or set lists for advertising.

Who Sees Your Content

Your songs and set lists are private to your account. Other users cannot view them. We may access stored data only as needed to operate, secure, or support the App, or as required by law.

Service Providers

We rely on third parties to run the App. They process data only to provide their services:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage of your account, songs, and set lists.
  • Stripe — processes Pro subscription payments, collects your payment details, and tells us whether your subscription is active. Your email address is passed to Stripe at checkout.
  • Netlify — hosting and delivery of the App, which may log standard request data such as IP address and browser type.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser, which may receive your IP address when loading them.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and content until you delete it or ask us to. You can delete individual songs and saved set lists in the App at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email us at the address below and we will do so within a reasonable time. Clearing your browser data removes the on-device data described above.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access to the database is restricted by account. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise them, contact us below. We will not discriminate against you for doing so.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do, and continued use of the App after an update means you accept it.
